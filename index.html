Vescent Matching Tool

A starting point for the Vescent team.

Start matching

Enter the Google account you use for Vescent matching. This opens the tool with that account selected, even when you have other Google accounts signed in.

Use the email approved for the matching tool. Your Chrome profile name may be different from the Google account used by the tool.

If Google asks for permission, check the email shown before continuing. If it keeps asking after approval, try a private browser window signed in only to your approved account.

The staff tool provides match suggestions and saves your decisions and team history.

Make the actual assignment in the admin portal, then confirm it in the staff tool.
